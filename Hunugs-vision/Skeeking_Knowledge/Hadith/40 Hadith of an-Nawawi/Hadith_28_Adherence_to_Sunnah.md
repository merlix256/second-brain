# Hadith 28: Adherence to Sunnah: Clinging with Molar Teeth
### لزوم السنة والتحذير من البدع

> **Narrated by:** Al-Irbad ibn Sariyah (رضي الله عنه)  
> **Canonical Sources:** Sunan Abi Dawud 4607, Jami` at-Tirmidhi 2676

---

## 📜 Complete Prophetic Text

**عَنْ أَبِي نَجِيحٍ الْعِرْبَاضِ بْنِ سَارِيَةَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: وَعَظَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم مَوْعِظَةً وَجِلَتْ مِنْهَا الْقُلُوبُ، وَذَرَفَتْ مِنْهَا الْعُيُونُ، فَقُلْنَا: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ! كَأَنَّهَا مَوْعِظَةُ مُوَدِّعٍ فَأَوْصِنَا، قَالَ: «أُوصِيكُمْ بِتَقْوَى اللَّهِ، وَالسَّمْعِ وَالطَّاعَةِ وَإِنْ تَأَمَّرَ عَلَيْكُمْ عَبْدٌ، فَإِنَّهُ مَنْ يَعِشْ مِنْكُمْ فَسَيَرَى اخْتِلَافًا كَثِيرًا، فَعَلَيْكُمْ بِسُنَّتِي وَسُنَّةِ الْخُلَفَاءِ الرَّاشِدِينَ الْمَهْدِيينَ، عَضُّوا عَلَيْهَا بِالنَّوَاجِذِ، وَإِيَّاكُمْ وَمُحْدَثَاتِ الْأُمُورِ؛ فَإِنَّ كُلَّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلَالَةٌ».**

> *"On the authority of Abu Najih al-'Irbad ibn Sariyah (ra) who said: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) gave us a profound sermon by which hearts trembled and eyes wept. So we said: "O Messenger of Allah, it is as though this is a farewell sermon, so counsel us." He said: "I counsel you to have Taqwa of Allah, and to listen and obey even if an enslaved person were appointed over you. For indeed, he who lives long among you will witness great controversy. So adhere to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs; bite onto it with your molar teeth! And beware of newly invented matters, for every innovation is misguidance.""*

## 💡 In-Depth Sharh & Classical Commentary

### The Survival Guide for Eras of Confusion
- **The Poignancy of the Farewell:** Sermons that evoke tears and awe imprint deeply upon the psyche. The Prophet (ﷺ) paired spiritual consciousness (*Taqwa*) with civic discipline.
- **The Metaphor of Molar Teeth:** When carrying something precious while hands are occupied or slipping on a cliff, biting down with molars represents the fiercest refusal to let go of principle.
