#### 🌟 Classical Principles of Hadith Study
1. **Grounded Verification (Tahqiq):** Approach sacred texts through authentic canonical chains and scholarly consensus.
2. **Actionable Knowledge ('Ilm Nafi'):** Study hadiths not as historical abstractions, but as living algorithms that recalibrate behavior, character, and work ethic.
3. **Internalizing Ihsan:** Embody perfection of craft, scrupulous honesty in transactions, and compassion toward all creation.
