# Hadith 04: Creation in the Womb and Destiny (Qadar)
### مراحل خلق الجنين والقدر

> **Narrated by:** Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (رضي الله عنه)  
> **Canonical Sources:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3208, Sahih Muslim 2643

---

## 📜 Complete Prophetic Text

**عَنْ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ مَسْعُودٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: حَدَّثَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم -وَهُوَ الصَّادِقُ الْمَصْدُوقُ-: «إِنَّ أَحَدَكُمْ يُجْمَعُ خَلْقُهُ فِي بَطْنِ أُمِّهِ أَرْبَعِينَ يَوْمًا نُطْفَةً، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ عَلَقَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يَكُونُ مُضْغَةً مِثْلَ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ يُرْسَلُ إِلَيْهِ الْمَلَكُ فَيَنْفُخُ فِيهِ الرُّوحَ، وَيُؤْمَرُ بِأَرْبَعِ كَلِمَاتٍ: بِكَتْبِ رِزْقِهِ، وَأَجَلِهِ، وَعَمَلِهِ، وَشَقِيٌّ أَمْ سَعِيدٌ...»**

> *"On the authority of Abu Abdur-Rahman Abdullah ibn Mas'ud (ra) who said: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), and he is the truthful, the confirmed, narrated to us: "Verily, the creation of each one of you is assembled in his mother's womb for forty days as a drop (nutfah), then he becomes a clinging clot ('alaqah) for a like period, then a lump of flesh (mudghah) for a like period. Then the angel is sent to him and breathes the spirit into him, and is commanded with four matters: to write down his provision (rizq), his lifespan (ajal), his deeds ('amal), and whether he will be wretched or blessed...""*

## 💡 In-Depth Sharh & Classical Commentary

### Stages of Human Genesis and Serenity of Heart
- **Embryological Precision:** Centuried before modern embryology, the Prophet (ﷺ) described development through successive forty-day intervals matching Quranic revelation (Surah al-Hajj 22:5, Surah al-Mu'minun 23:12-14).
- **Certainty of Sustenance (Rizq):** Knowing that your lifetime provision was decreed before your birth liberates the mind from paralyzing fear. No competitor can deprive you of a dollar ordained for you.
- **Emphasis on Endings:** The concluding warning underscores humility; a person should never become arrogant regarding current pious habits, but continuously pray for steadfastness (*Husn al-Khatimah*).

## ⚡ Practical & Productivity Application

### Productivity & Mental Resilience
- **Overcoming Scarcity Mindset:** Anxiety regarding job security or business contracts diminishes when one internalizes that Rizq is secured by divine decree. One focuses completely on high-quality effort (*Asbab*) while keeping the heart anchored in trust (*Tawakkul*).
