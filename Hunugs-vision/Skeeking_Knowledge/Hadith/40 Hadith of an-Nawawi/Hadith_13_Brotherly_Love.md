# Hadith 13: Brotherhood and Empathy: Loving for Others
### حب الخير للغير من كمال الإيمان

> **Narrated by:** Anas ibn Malik (رضي الله عنه)  
> **Canonical Sources:** Sahih al-Bukhari 13, Sahih Muslim 45

---

## 📜 Complete Prophetic Text

**عَنْ أَبِي حَمْزَةَ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ خَادِمِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه و سلم عَنْ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه و سلم قَالَ: «لَا يُؤْمِنُ أَحَدُكُمْ حَتَّى يُحِبَّ لِأَخِيهِ مَا يُحِبُّ لِنَفْسِهِ».**

> *"On the authority of Abu Hamzah Anas bin Malik (ra), the servant of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), that the Prophet (ﷺ) said: "None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.""*

---

## 💡 In-Depth Sharh & Classical Commentary

### Eradicating Envy (*Hasad*) and Cultivating Universal Empathy
- **Negation of Perfection:** Scholars emphasize this signifies the perfection of faith. A heart harboring malicious envy (*Hasad*) or wishing others to fail cannot experience the sweetness of Iman.
- **Universal Humanity:** Al-Hafidh Ibn Rajab noted that *"his brother"* encompasses both the brother in Islam (desiring guidance and piety) and the brother in humanity (desiring guidance and general welfare).
