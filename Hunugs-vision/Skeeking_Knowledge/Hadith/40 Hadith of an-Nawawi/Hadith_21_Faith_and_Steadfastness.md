# Hadith 21: Faith and Steadfastness (Istiqamah)
### الاستقامة بعد الإيمان

> **Narrated by:** Sufyan ibn Abdullah ath-Thaqafi (رضي الله عنه)  
> **Canonical Sources:** Sahih Muslim 38

---

## 📜 Complete Prophetic Text

**عَنْ أَبِي عَمْرٍو وَقِيلَ: أَبِي عَمْرَةَ سُفْيَانَ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ قَالَ: قُلْت: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ! قُلْ لِي فِي الْإِسْلَامِ قَوْلًا لَا أَسْأَلُ عَنْهُ أَحَدًا غَيْرَك؛ قَالَ: «قُلْ: آمَنْت بِاللَّهِ ثُمَّ اسْتَقِمْ».**

> *"On the authority of Abu 'Amr—and he is also called Abu 'Amrah—Sufyan bin Abdullah ath-Thaqafi (ra) who said: I said: "O Messenger of Allah, tell me something about Islam so comprehensive that I will not need to ask anyone after you." He (ﷺ) replied: "Say: 'I believe in Allah'—and then remain steadfast.""*

## 💡 In-Depth Sharh & Classical Commentary

### The Twin Pillars of Salvation
- **Comprehensive Synthesis:** The Quran confirms: *"Verily, those who say: 'Our Lord is Allah', and thereafter stand firm and straight..."* (Surah Fussilat 41:30). Faith provides the coordinates; *Istiqamah* (steadfastness) is the execution along the trajectory.
- **The Reality of Istiqamah:** Abu Bakr as-Siddiq defined it as never associating anything with Allah; Umar ibn al-Khattab defined it as adhering straightly to commandments and prohibitions without dodging like a fox.

---

## ⚡ Practical & Productivity Application

### Productivity & Compounding Consistency
- **The Mastery of Continuous Execution:** Spectacular bursts of motivation mean little without steady daily execution. Whether mastering an engineering domain, learning a language, or building spiritual presence, *Istiqamah* (consistency) is the engine of compounding progress.
