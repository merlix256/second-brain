# 02. Supplications for Barakah in Time, Effort, and Sustenance

Duas to remove friction, overcome procrastination, unlock productivity, and invite divine blessings (Barakah) into your time, career, and daily tasks.

---

### 1. Protection Against Procrastination, Laziness, and Overwhelm

> **اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma innī a‘ūdhu bika minal-hammi wal-ḥazan, wal-‘ajzi wal-kasal, wal-bukhli wal-jubn, wa ḍala‘id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijāl.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and sorrow, from inability and laziness, from miserliness and cowardice, from being overwhelmed by debt and overpowered by people."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sahih al-Bukhari 2893, 5425.
- **✨ Barakah & Productivity Application:** 
  - *Al-Hamm* is anxiety regarding what is to come; *Al-Hazan* is grief over past regrets.
  - *Al-‘Ajz* is physical or mental incapacity; *Al-Kasal* is lack of motivation despite having capacity.
  - Recite this in the morning to shatter inertia and tackle demanding engineering problems.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Overcoming Anxiety and Laziness by Ustadh Nouman Ali Khan](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Nouman+Ali+Khan+Allahumma+Inni+Audhubika+Minal+Hammi+Wal+Hazan).

### 2. The Post-Fajr Productivity Triad: Knowledge, Rizq, and Acceptance

> **اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلاً مُتَقَبَّلاً**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma innī as’aluka ‘ilman nāfi‘an, wa rizqan ṭayyiban, wa ‘amalan mutaqabbalā.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, wholesome (pure) sustenance, and accepted deeds."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sunan Ibn Majah 925; Musnad Ahmad 26521 (Classed Sahih by Al-Albani).
- **✨ Barakah & Productivity Application:** Recited every day immediately following the morning prayer (Fajr). It aligns your intent for study, programming, or work with pure nourishment and divine acceptance.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [The 3 Morning Goals by Dr. Omar Suleiman](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+Inni+As%27aluka+Ilman+Nafian+Omar+Suleiman).

### 3. Financial Freedom and Sufficiency in Halal

> **اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلاَلِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ، وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhummak-finī bi-ḥalālika ‘an ḥarāmik, wa aghninī bi-faḍlika ‘amman siwāk.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, suffice me with Your lawful provisions against what You have forbidden, and enrich me by Your grace so that I am independent of all others."*
- **📖 Reference:** Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3563 (Classed Hasan).
- **✨ Barakah & Productivity Application:** Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA) advised a man under heavy debt to recite this dua. It opens doors of honest income and grants barakah in engineering contracts, career growth, and savings.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Dua for Debt and Financial Freedom](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+ikfini+bi+halalika+an+haramika+Mufti+Menk).

### 4. Overcoming Difficulty and Finding Ease in Complex Work

> **اللَّهُمَّ لاَ سَهْلَ إِلاَّ مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلاً، وَأَنْتَ تَجْعَلُ الْحَزْنَ إِذَا شِئْتَ سَهْلاً**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma lā sahla illā mā ja‘altahu sahlā, wa Anta taj‘alul-ḥazna idhā shi’ta sahlā.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, there is no ease except that which You make easy, and You turn sorrow and difficulty into ease if You will."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sahih Ibn Hibban 974; classed Sahih according to the conditions of Muslim by Al-Albani (*As-Silsilah As-Sahihah* 2886).
- **✨ Barakah & Productivity Application:** Essential when debugging thorny code, tackling complex mathematical proofs, or preparing for exams and interviews.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Dua for Overcoming Difficulties](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+la+sahla+illa+ma+ja%27altahu+sahla+explanation).

### 5. Seeking Goodness in Moments of Need (Musa's Dua)

> **رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ**

- **Transliteration:** *Rabbi innī limā anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqīr.*
- **Translation:** *"My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in dire need."*
- **📖 Reference:** Surah Al-Qasas (28:24).
- **✨ Barakah & Productivity Application:** Prophet Musa (AS) recited this while exhausted, homeless, and empty-handed in Madyan. Immediately afterward, Allah granted him shelter, honorable work, and a righteous spouse. Recite this when seeking jobs, guidance, or project opportunities.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [The Dua that Changed Musa's Life by Nouman Ali Khan](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Nouman+Ali+Khan+Rabbi+Inni+Lima+Anzalta+Ilayya).
