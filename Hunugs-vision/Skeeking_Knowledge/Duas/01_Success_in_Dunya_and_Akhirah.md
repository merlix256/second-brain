# 01. Supplications for Success in Dunya and Akhirah

Comprehensive supplications that harmonize worldly excellence (health, meaningful achievements, dignity) with eternal salvation in the hereafter.

---

### 1. The Most Comprehensive Quranic Dua (The Balance of Life)

> **رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ**

- **Transliteration:** *Rabbanā ātinā fid-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fil-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ‘adhāban-nār.*
- **Translation:** *"Our Lord, give us in this world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire."*
- **📖 Reference:** Surah Al-Baqarah (2:201); Sahih al-Bukhari 6389.
- **✨ Barakah & Application:** Imam Ibn Kathir explained that "hasanah in the dunya" encompasses beneficial knowledge, righteous deeds, permissible sustenance, physical vitality, peace of mind, and harmonious relationships.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [The Most Comprehensive Dua by Dr. Omar Suleiman](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Omar+Suleiman+Rabbana+Atina+Fid+Dunya+Hasanah) (Yaqeen Institute).

### 2. Rectifying All Dimensions of Life

> **اللَّهُمَّ أَصْلِحْ لِي دِينِي الَّذِي هُوَ عِصْمَةُ أَمْرِي، وَأَصْلِحْ لِي دُنْيَايَ الَّتِي فِيهَا مَعَاشِي، وَأَصْلِحْ لِي آخِرَتِي الَّتِي فِيهَا مَعَادِي، وَاجْعَلِ الْحَيَاةَ زِيَادَةً لِي فِي كُلِّ خَيْرٍ، وَاجْعَلِ الْمَوْتَ رَاحَةً لِي مِنْ كُلِّ شَرٍّ**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma aṣliḥ lī dīnī alladhī huwa ‘iṣmatu amrī, wa aṣliḥ lī dunyāya allatī fīhā ma‘āshī, wa aṣliḥ lī ākhiratī allatī fīhā ma‘ādī, waj‘alil-ḥayāta ziyādatan lī fī kulli khayr, waj‘alil-mawta rāḥatan lī min kulli sharr.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, rectify for me my religion which is the safeguard of my affairs; rectify for me my world in which is my livelihood; rectify for me my Hereafter to which is my return; make my life an increase in all good, and make death a relief for me from all evil."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2720.
- **✨ Barakah & Application:** Perfect for organizing priorities. It requests that professional work and engineering endeavors serve spiritual balance rather than distract from it.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Rectify My Affairs Dua Explanation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+aslih+li+dini+alladhi+huwa+ismatu+amri+explanation).

### 3. Seeking Guidance, Piety, Chastity, and Self-Sufficiency

> **اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma innī as’alukal-hudā wat-tuqā wal-‘afāfa wal-ghinā.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, restraint (chastity), and self-sufficiency."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2721.
- **✨ Barakah & Application:** Al-Ghina (independence of soul and sufficiency of means) liberates a professional from dependency on anyone other than Allah, enabling ethical choices in business and career.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [The 4 Essentials of Life by Mufti Menk](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+inni+as%27aluka+al-huda+wat-tuqa+Mufti+Menk).

### 4. Comprehensive Well-Being (Al-‘Afiyah)

> **اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ، اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْعَفْوَ وَالْعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma innī as’alukal-‘afwa wal-‘āfiyata fid-dunyā wal-ākhirah. Allāhumma innī as’alukal-‘afwa wal-‘āfiyata fī dīnī wa dunyāya wa ahlī wa mālī.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in this world and the Hereafter. O Allah, I ask You for pardon and well-being in my religion, my worldly life, my family, and my wealth."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sunan Abi Dawud 5074; Sunan Ibn Majah 3871 (Classed Sahih by Al-Albani).
- **✨ Barakah & Application:** The Prophet ﷺ taught that after certainty of faith (Yaqeen), no gift is greater than Al-‘Afiyah (protection from physical, mental, moral, and spiritual afflictions).
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Why the Prophet Asked for Afiyah Every Day](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=The+Power+of+Afiyah+Yaqeen+Institute).

### 5. Seeking Divine Reform and Non-Reliance on the Self

> **يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ، أَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ، وَلاَ تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ**

- **Transliteration:** *Yā Ḥayyu yā Qayyūm, bi-raḥmatika astaghīth, aṣliḥ lī sha’nī kullah, wa lā takilnī ilā nafsī ṭarfata ‘ayn.*
- **Translation:** *"O Ever-Living, O Sustainer of all existence, by Your mercy I seek assistance. Rectify for me all of my affairs, and do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sunan an-Nasa'i (Al-Sunan al-Kubra 10405); Mustadrak al-Hakim 1/545 (Classed Sahih).
- **✨ Barakah & Application:** A powerful mindset reset before complex technical tasks, engineering projects, or high-stakes decisions.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Do Not Leave Me to Myself - Dua Explanation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Ya+Hayyu+Ya+Qayyum+bi+rahmatika+astagheeth+explanation).
