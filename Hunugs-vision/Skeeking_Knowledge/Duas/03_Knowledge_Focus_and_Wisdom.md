# 03. Supplications for Knowledge, Mental Clarity, and Wisdom

Supplications for memory retention, clear speech, technical mastery, and humility in learning.

---

### 1. The Quranic Injunction to Increase in Knowledge

> **رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا**

- **Transliteration:** *Rabbi zidnī ‘ilmā.*
- **Translation:** *"My Lord, increase me in knowledge."*
- **📖 Reference:** Surah Ta-Ha (20:114).
- **✨ Application:** The only thing Allah commanded the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ to explicitly ask for more of in the Quran is *'Ilm* (knowledge). Use this before reading technical literature, attending lectures, or studying system architecture.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Rabbi Zidni Ilma Reflection by Ustadh Nouman Ali Khan](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Nouman+Ali+Khan+Rabbi+Zidni+Ilma).

### 2. Clarity of Mind, Speech, and Confidence (Musa's Supplication)

> **رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي، وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي، وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي، يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي**

- **Transliteration:** *Rabbish-raḥ lī ṣadrī, wa yassir lī amrī, waḥlul ‘uqdatam-mil-lisānī, yafqahū qawlī.*
- **Translation:** *"My Lord, expand for me my chest [with assurance], ease for me my task, and untie the knot from my tongue so they may understand my speech."*
- **📖 Reference:** Surah Ta-Ha (20:25-28).
- **✨ Application:** Essential before technical presentations, defending engineering theses, interviews, or difficult conversations with stakeholders.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Overcoming Fear and Public Speaking](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Rabbishrah+li+sadri+Bayyinah).

### 3. Turning Theory into Practical Benefit

> **اللَّهُمَّ انْفَعْنِي بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنِي، وَعَلِّمْنِي مَا يَنْفَعُنِي، وَزِدْنِي عِلْمًا**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumman-fa‘nī bimā ‘allamtanī, wa ‘allimnī mā yanfa‘unī, wa zidnī ‘ilmā.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, benefit me through that which You have taught me, teach me that which will benefit me, and increase me in knowledge."*
- **📖 Reference:** Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3599; Sunan Ibn Majah 251 (Classed Sahih by Al-Albani).
- **✨ Application:** Prevents "tutorial hell" and aimless consumption of information. It directs learning toward real-world benefit and service to humanity.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Beneficial Knowledge vs Information Overload](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumman+fani+bima+allamtani+explanation).

### 4. Protection Against Non-Beneficial Knowledge & A Distracted Heart

> **اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عِلْمٍ لاَ يَنْفَعُ، وَمِنْ قَلْبٍ لاَ يَخْشَعُ، وَمِنْ نَفْسٍ لاَ تَشْبَعُ، وَمِنْ دَعْوَةٍ لاَ يُسْتَجَابُ لَهَا**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma innī a‘ūdhu bika min ‘ilmin lā yanfa‘, wa min qalbin lā yakhsha‘, wa min nafsin lā tashba‘, wa min da‘watin lā yustajābu lahā.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from knowledge that does not benefit, from a heart that lacks reverence, from a soul that is never satisfied, and from a supplication that is not answered."*
- **📖 Reference:** Sahih Muslim 2722.
- **✨ Application:** Guards against analysis paralysis, superficial distractions, information overload, and insatiable greed.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [The 4 Things the Prophet Feared](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Knowledge+that+does+not+benefit+Yaqeen+Institute).

---

### 5. Sound Judgment and Protection from Ego

> **اللَّهُمَّ أَلْهِمْنِي رُشْدِي، وَأَعِذْنِي مِنْ شَرِّ نَفْسِي**

- **Transliteration:** *Allāhumma alhimnī rushdī, wa a‘idhnī min sharri nafsī.*
- **Translation:** *"O Allah, inspire me with sound guidance and judgment, and protect me from the evil of my own self."*
- **📖 Reference:** Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3483 (Classed Hasan).
- **✨ Application:** Promotes intellectual humility, helps overcome confirmation bias, and protects against the traps of ego and arrogance when gaining expertise.
- **🎥 Video Insight:** [Inspire Me with Sound Guidance](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Allahumma+alhimni+rushdi+explanation).
