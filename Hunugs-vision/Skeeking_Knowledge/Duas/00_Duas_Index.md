## 📚 Principles of Dua for Productivity & Barakah

1. **Yaqeen (Certainty):** Make supplication with firm conviction that Allah answers according to what is best for you.
2. **Taking the Means (Al-Akhdh bil-Asbab):** Dua is paired with deep, focused, disciplined effort.
3. **Taharah of Intent:** Align daily work, studies, and engineering projects as acts of service and worship.
