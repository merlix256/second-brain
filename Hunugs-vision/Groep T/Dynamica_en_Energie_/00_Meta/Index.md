## 🗂️ Core Chapters & Curriculum Map

### Deel I: Kinematica van een Puntmassa (Kinematics of Particles)
- [[Chapter 1 - Constante Versnelling en Cirkelbeweging]] — *Rectilinear motion with constant acceleration, free fall, projectile motion, and uniform circular kinematics*.
- [[Chapter 2 - Variabele Versnelling]] — *Kinematics with variable acceleration, differential relationships ($a\,ds = v\,dv$), and path-dependent motion*.
- [[Chapter 3 - Afhankelijke en Relatieve Beweging]] — *Pulley cable systems, kinematic constraints, and relative velocity/acceleration vectors*.

### Deel II: Kinetica van een Puntmassa (Kinetics of Particles: Force & Acceleration)
- [[Chapter 4 - Kinetica Newton Rectangulair]] — *Newton's 2nd Law ($\sum \mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$), Cartesian coordinate systems, and Free Body Diagrams (VLS)*.
- [[Chapter 5 - Kinetica Newton t-n Stelsels]] — *Equations of motion in intrinsic normal-tangential ($t-n-b$) coordinates, centripetal forces, and radius of curvature*.

### Deel III: Arbeid, Energie en Behoudswetten (Work, Energy & Momentum)
- [[Chapter 6 - Arbeid en Energie]] — *Work of variable/constant forces, spring forces, gravitational work, and the work-energy principle ($T_1 + \sum U_{1-2} = T_2$)*.
- [[Chapter 7 - Behoud van Energie]] — *Conservative force fields, potential energy functions ($V_g, V_e$), and total mechanical energy conservation*.
- [[Chapter 8 - Lineair Momentum en Stoot]] — *Principle of linear impulse and momentum, conservation of momentum, and particle collisions*.
