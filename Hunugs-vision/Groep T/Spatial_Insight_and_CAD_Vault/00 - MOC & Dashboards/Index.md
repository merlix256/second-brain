## 🔄 The 4-Step Learning Cycle

1. **Watch & Note**: Complete the designated Toledo [[Kennisclips Dashboard|Kennisclips]] (fill in notes and active recall prompts).
2. **Read Coursebook**: Deepen understanding with [[Chapter 02 - Orthogonale Aanzichten (Multiview Projections)]] and [[Chapter 03 - Doorsnedeaanzichten (Section Views)]].
3. **Execute Oefeningen**: Attend the 2-hour campus exercise session and complete at least 2 hours of home drawing practice ([[Exercise Session Tracker]]).
4. **Formative Assessment**: Verify spatial reasoning with [[ANS Tests Self-Assessment Tracker|ANS Tests (1, 2, 3)]] and review correction keys.
